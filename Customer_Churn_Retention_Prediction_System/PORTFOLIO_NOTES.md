# Portfolio Notes

**Project:** Customer Churn & Retention Prediction System

**Domain:** Churn Prediction

**Problem solved:** Predict customer churn risk and turn model probabilities into actionable retention priorities.

**What was built:** End-to-end Python ML pipeline using preprocessing, categorical encoding, scaling, Logistic Regression and Random Forest, model evaluation, customer risk scoring, revenue-at-risk estimation and retention targeting.

**Key result 1:** Random Forest ROC-AUC = 0.831; PR-AUC = 0.633 on the held-out test set.

**Key result 2:** 1907 customers fall into the high-risk probability tier (>=60%) under the project's scoring policy.

**Tools:** Python, pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, matplotlib, joblib, Jupyter.

**Business output:** customer risk scores, retention targets, feature importance and estimated monthly revenue exposure.
